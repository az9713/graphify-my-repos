# Your Work Proves Who You Are

## About

This interactive experience distills Alex Li's architecture-to-design-engineering portfolio into practical lessons on showing process, technical range, iteration, measurable impact, and tangible outcomes. It is inspired by **[The Design Portfolio Got Me Into Harvard + Berkeley (LEAVING ARCHITECTURE)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0rzwzmAx9Vs&t=420s)**.
