# AI for Materials Discovery

Notes, takeaways, and a comprehensive tutorial on AI-driven materials discovery — based on the [Latent Space](https://www.youtube.com/@LatentSpaceTV) podcast interview with **Prof. Heather Kulik** (Chemical Engineering, MIT).

## Source

This repository is built from the Latent Space YouTube podcast episode featuring Prof. Heather Kulik discussing how AI and machine learning are transforming materials science — from polymer toughness discovery to multi-objective optimization of metal-organic frameworks for CO2 capture.

Watch the full episode on the [Latent Space YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@LatentSpaceTV).

## Topics Covered

- Quantum mechanical modeling and Density Functional Theory (DFT)
- Transition metal catalysis and ligand design
- Metal-organic frameworks (MOFs) for CO2 capture
- Machine learning surrogates for materials property prediction
- Active learning for multi-objective optimization
- Machine-learned interatomic potentials and foundation models
- Literature extraction with NLP and LLMs
- The bits-to-atoms gap and autonomous labs
- AlphaFold vs. materials science — why an "AlphaFold for materials" is harder
- Open problems and opportunities for ML engineers
