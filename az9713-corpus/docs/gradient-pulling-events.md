# Gradient Pulling Events

Two model responses to the same prompt:

> **"Uncover / surface overlooked or subconscious 'gradient pulling' events in humans' daily lives."**

"Gradient pulling" borrows the ML metaphor of an optimizer being pulled down the steepest slope of a landscape: subtle, sub-conscious forces in our environment, body, and mind that tilt the floor under us so we drift in directions we never consciously chose. Each is trivial; consistently applied, they integrate into large life displacements.

## Summary of the Opus 4.8 output

`gradient-pulling-events.md` defines the concept, then surfaces the *non-obvious* pulls across seven domains — attention/digital, physical/spatial, social, language/framing, time, body, and money (e.g. the default sort order of any list, the "six feet of friction" of a snack on the counter, whoever-speaks-first anchoring, hunger reshaping judgment, auto-renew inertia). Its central claim: **you can't beat a gradient with willpower — you reshape the landscape** so the slope points where you already want to go. It closes with a three-question audit for spotting your own.
